### 💡 THINKING

---

**01 / Problem first.**  
I have shipped the wrong solution before because I fell in love with a tool instead of the problem it was meant to solve.

<br>

**02 / Experience over complexity.**  
A simpler solution that people can actually use beats an elegant one nobody understands.

<br>

**03 / Consistency wins.**  
Good interfaces are often defined by the details: spacing, typography, interaction, and consistency.
